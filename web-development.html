FAQ

Web Development FAQs

A few things you may want to know before starting your project.

How long does it take to build a website?
The timeline depends on the number of pages, design requirements, content, and functionality. Once we understand your project, we can provide a realistic schedule.
Will my website work on mobile devices?
Yes. Responsive development is part of our approach, so the layout is designed to adapt to mobile phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We can review your current website, understand what needs improvement, and help refresh its design, structure, usability, and functionality.
Do I need to provide the website content?
You can provide your text, images, and brand materials if you have them. If you need help organizing the content for your pages, we can discuss that during planning.
Can I request changes during development?
Yes. We keep communication open throughout the project and review feedback at agreed stages so the finished website aligns with your requirements.
How do I get started?
Send us a project inquiry using the form below. Tell us a little about your business and what you want your website to achieve, and we can take it from there.
